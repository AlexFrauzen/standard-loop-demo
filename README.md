**Rules implemented from the [public whitepaper v0.1](https://www.standardreserve.xyz/whitepaper/)**

- §§6–8: one charter starts with one branch and holds at most 10. Initially 100 licenses are available per day, at most 3 per charter. Existing purchases and free branch slots constrain purchases. Last-branch retirement burns the charter.
- §§5, 7: each branch has an equal issuance share. Interval credit is `I × your branches / total branches`; Sandbox issuance is `base × epoch days × multiplier`. The snapshot buys licenses before a fixed-count interval and retires branches afterward. License payment is separate from the accrued ledger balance; automated reinvestment and intermediate timing are not assumed.
- §§3, 9: accrual creates ledger credit, not tokens. Retirement releases `balance × retired branches / held branches`. The wallet receives that amount less the entered fee. Half the fee burns and half is allocated to remaining bankers. Individual redistribution weights and gross fee-mint accounting are not inferred.
- §§7–8: license payments burn 100%. Auction price is `start × (floor / start)^(hours / 24)`. License opens are 2× the previous sale; charter opens are 3×. If no sales occurred, the floor is used instead. Auctions close at 24 hours or when sold out; unsold inventory does not roll over. Charter auctions initially have a daily count of zero, and unsold charters are not minted.
- §§4–5: current net flow is gross ETH buys minus gross ETH sells. Positive flow routes fees to expansion; zero or negative routes to contraction. The issuance policy signal uses the sum of the two previous completed epochs. Its multiplier transition remains uncomputed.
- §11: trading-fee ETH plus charter-auction ETH splits 70% to the active vault, 15% to permanent liquidity and 15% to the team. Half of the liquidity allocation is for the token swap, half for ETH pairing. Expansion accumulates reserves; contraction buys and burns. The independent hourly buyback limit is `min(10% × vault ETH, 0.2% × pool reserve ETH)`; unspent ETH remains.
- §§3, 9: the disclosed cap is 1B STANDARD, with 100M genesis liquidity and a 900M issuance budget. Interval issuance cannot exceed the remaining budget and stops at exhaustion. Exit pressure uses `W / max(D + W, entered minimum)`, where W covers seven days.
- Explicit values elsewhere in the paper take precedence over duplicate redactions in the launch table. Decorative SVG coordinates and redaction widths are ignored. This is an offline snapshot calculator using JavaScript numbers, not exact 18-decimal transaction arithmetic.

TOY Low / TOY Mid / TOY High fill only labelled examples and clear unpublished inputs.

- Unpublished multiplier floor, ceiling, initial value, cut rule, raise rule/condition, durations to full issuance/ceiling/floor, and dilution reduction. These are blank reference fields; no hidden transition equation or date is invented.
- Unpublished license floor, charter reserve floor and enabled daily charter count. “About two days of yield” is not treated as an exact floor formula. A blank previous sale is unknown; a day without sales must be selected explicitly.
- Unpublished committed exit fee, pressure denominator minimum, curve floor, ceiling and saturation threshold. The committed fee is entered directly because its exact equation is hidden. Curve parameters are reference inputs only. System-wide withdrawals and held balances are manual observations.
- Unpublished trading-fee rate and other redacted launch settings have blank reference fields. Fee proceeds are entered as ETH amounts because their precise rate calculation is not specified. Hidden illustrative fee-table points do not become independent rules. Dormancy and future transfers are outside this loop calculator.
- Every unpublished field reads “not published — your input”. Blank stays unknown; zero must be entered explicitly. Missing dependencies leave results unresolved. No STANDARD/ETH conversion, price forecast, APR, launch date or contract address is provided.
